# Research and software notebooks

Location `research/notebooks/` · [Up to research/](../RESEARCH_GUIDE.md)

| Notebook | Inputs | What it demonstrates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [01_complete_system_pipeline.ipynb](01_complete_system_pipeline.ipynb) | Local PNG or JPEG scans and the application modules. The walkthrough uses a synthetic segmentation adapter. | Follows the six interfaces from request and image to triage, optional retrieval and display. |
